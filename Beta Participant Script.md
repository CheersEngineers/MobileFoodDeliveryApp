## Quick brief (2 minutes)
You will act as a typical customer. Please think aloud while you perform tasks. We are testing the app, not you — honest feedback is valuable.

## Tasks (guided)
1. **Search for restaurants**
   - Open the app and go to Search.
   - Enter **cuisine = Japanese**, **location = Downtown**, **rating = 4.0**.
   - Tap Search.
   - **What to observe:** Are results relevant? Any confusing labels? Take a screenshot of results.

2. **Search with no filters**
   - Clear all filters and run Search.
   - **What to observe:** Are all restaurants shown? Is the list usable?

3. **Schedule a future order**
   - Add an item to cart (use provided test account if needed).
   - Choose Schedule Delivery and pick a date **2 days from now at 18:00**.
   - Confirm the order.
   - **What to observe:** Did you get a confirmation? Does the Orders list show the scheduled order?

4. **Attempt to schedule outside allowed window**
   - Try to schedule 30 days ahead.
   - **What to observe:** Is there a clear validation message? Is scheduling blocked?

## Exploratory time (10 minutes)
Try other flows you find interesting (filter combinations, sorting, tapping restaurant details). Note anything that surprises you.
